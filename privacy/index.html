EBB

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Everything you put into Ebb stays on your iPhone. Ebb has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads.

What Ebb keeps

Ebb keeps what you give it: journal entries, voice recordings and their transcripts, photos you add, your decisions and the choices you log, pursuits, routines, and what Ebb learns from them, such as the time of day a decision tends to be tested. All of it is stored on your iPhone, inside the app. The developer can't see it, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions, and what each one is for

  • Microphone and speech recognition. These turn what you say into text for voice entries. Ebb asks your iPhone to do this on the device. On a phone that can't, Apple's speech service may process the audio, under Apple's privacy policy. Recordings are saved on your iPhone, and you can delete them in Settings.
  • Camera and photos. Ebb only sees the photos you take or choose to add to an entry. Handwritten pages are read on the device with Apple's Vision framework.
  • Calendar and reminders. Only if you choose to import them. Ebb reads them on the device to fill in your day.
  • Screen Time. This is used when you protect a decision or a block of focus. Apple only gives Ebb anonymous tokens for the apps you choose to shield, so Ebb never learns which apps they are. Nothing about your app use leaves the device.
  • Notifications. These are scheduled on your iPhone. They never come daily, and they ease off as you go. Lock-screen details are off unless you turn them on.

Intelligence

The questions, patterns and ideas Ebb suggests come from rules that run on your iPhone. Where Ebb uses a language model, it is Apple's on-device model, so your words aren't sent anywhere to be processed.

Backups

Like any app's data, Ebb's is included in your iPhone backup, to iCloud or to a computer, if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under its privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → "Reset what Ebb has learned" clears the patterns Ebb has picked up.
  • Settings → "Delete Audio Files" removes your voice recordings and keeps their transcripts.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything Ebb stored on your iPhone.

Testing with TestFlight

If you test Ebb through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares some information with the developer: the name and email you were invited with, or nothing beyond install counts if you joined through a public link; crash reports; and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. None of this includes what you wrote in Ebb. See Apple's TestFlight terms.

Children

Ebb isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. As above, it doesn't collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Ebb ever starts sending anything off your iPhone, it will ask you first.
